Questions

The short answers.

What powers ACE?

ACE runs on Claude, the AI made by Anthropic. Your messages go to Claude and its answers come back to this page.

Does it cost anything?

There's no separate fee. ACE uses your own Claude account, so messages count toward your Claude plan's usage, the same as chatting on claude.ai.

Where are my chats saved?

In the browser you're using. They don't sync to other devices, and clearing your browser data deletes them. Attached images aren't kept after you close the page.

Can I change how ACE talks?

Yes. In the chat, open Customize ACE to change its name or give it standing instructions such as "keep answers short" or "reply in French".

Can I share ACE with friends?

Yes, from the Share menu on this page. Each person chats using their own Claude account and has their own saved chats.
